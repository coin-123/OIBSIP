A loving husband. A dedicated father. A true friend.

ABOUT

Who Was Emmanuel Okafor?

Emmanuel Okafor was born on March 12, 1985, in Enugu State, Nigeria. He was the first child in a family of four.

From a young age, he showed a strong desire to learn and make a positive difference in the world.

He completed his university education in 2008, with a degree in Computer Science, and went on to build a successful career in the technology industry.

He was known for his gentle spirit, his sense of humor, and his willingness to help others. Whether at work, in his community, or at home, Emmanuel always showed love, patience and faith.
